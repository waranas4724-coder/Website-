Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

PromptForge provides AI-generated prompts, images, and related creative content. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use this website.

Information We Collect

PromptForge may process information that you voluntarily provide when using protected administration or publisher features, such as a username used for authentication. The website may also process technical information required to operate sessions, security features, and the website itself.

How We Use Information

Information is used to operate and secure the website, provide requested features, manage content, maintain authenticated sessions, and improve reliability.

Cookies and Sessions

Protected areas may use a secure session cookie so that an authenticated administrator or publisher can remain signed in. Session information is used for authentication and security and is not used for advertising purposes by PromptForge.

Third-Party Services

PromptForge may use third-party services to provide website functionality, including Supabase for database services, Cloudinary for media storage and delivery, AI or content APIs for generating or discovering content, and Pinterest when the site owner enables Pinterest integration. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Pinterest Integration

If the site owner connects a Pinterest Business account, PromptForge can use the Pinterest API to publish website content as Pins and to access the Pinterest account information and boards needed for that integration. Pinterest access can be disconnected by the account owner.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as needed to operate the website, maintain content, provide requested features, meet security requirements, or comply with applicable obligations.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The latest version will always be available at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact method made available on the PromptForge website.
